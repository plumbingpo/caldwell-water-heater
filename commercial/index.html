Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
